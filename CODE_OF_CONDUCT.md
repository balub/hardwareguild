# Absurd Industries Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Absurd Industries is committed to a welcoming, respectful environment for everyone who works with us or participates in our open source community.

We welcome people regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, nationality, religion, age, disability, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, appearance, education, economic background, or level of experience.

## Who and where this covers

This Code applies to employees, founders, managers, contractors, interns, maintainers, contributors, and community participants. Seniority or decision-making authority does not exempt anyone.

It covers:

- Workplaces, remote work, meetings, business travel, and company events.
- Company communication channels and internal collaboration tools.
- Open source repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and community channels managed by Absurd Industries.
- Direct messages and other communications connected to work or community participation.
- Public activities where someone officially represents Absurd Industries or its projects.

## Expected behaviour

Treat people with consideration. Respect different perspectives, give constructive feedback, and take responsibility when your actions cause harm.

Challenge ideas and technical decisions without attacking people. Make room for questions, disagreement, and learning. Respect personal boundaries and requests to stop unwanted behaviour.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are unacceptable:

- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, stalking, threats, or violence.
- Discrimination, degrading stereotypes, personal attacks, or targeted humiliation.
- Unwanted sexual attention, sexual harassment, or inappropriate sexual content.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Impersonation or attempts to evade moderation.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or participates in an investigation.

Good-faith disagreement, reporting problems, and constructive criticism are welcome.

## Reporting a concern

Report concerns privately to **[primary contact name or role and email]**.

If that person is involved in the concern, has a conflict of interest, or you are uncomfortable approaching them, contact **[alternative contact name or role and separate email]**.

You may report something you experienced or witnessed. Include whatever information you can comfortably provide, such as what happened, when, and relevant messages. You do not need to confront the person involved or collect complete evidence before reporting.

Internal team members may use these contacts directly without first approaching their manager. These reporting routes do not prevent anyone from using other available workplace or external reporting channels.

## Handling reports fairly

Absurd Industries will take reports seriously, review them promptly and impartially, and give those involved a reasonable opportunity to provide relevant information.

Anyone with a conflict of interest must step aside from handling the report or deciding the outcome. Reports concerning leadership must be handled by someone independent of the person concerned.

Information will be shared only as needed to assess the concern, protect people, and take appropriate action. Complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

A good-faith report does not become misconduct because it cannot be substantiated.

## Responses and consequences

Responses will reflect the seriousness, impact, and repetition of the behaviour. They may include a warning, restrictions on participation, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from community spaces. Serious incidents may justify immediate action.

For internal team members, employment or contractual action will follow applicable law, agreements, and workplace procedures. Community moderation measures do not replace those procedures.

A person affected by a decision may request reconsideration through the alternative reporting contact, stating any relevant new information or concerns about the process. Where practicable, someone uninvolved in the original decision will review it.
